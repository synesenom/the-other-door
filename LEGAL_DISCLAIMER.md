recruiters and other people; treat them as personal data and keep them for your job search only.

## What Claude writes for you

*The other door* scores postings and drafts CVs and cover letters with Claude, through the `claude` command
line tool on your own plan: your CV and the postings are sent to Anthropic for that, and Anthropic's terms
apply to that use. Drafts can be wrong: *The other door* flags claims that your CV does not back, but you must
read and correct everything before you send it. *The other door* never submits an application; you do, and
you are responsible for what you send.

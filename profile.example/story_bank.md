## Halved the Month-End Close at Acme Corp

**Context**: The group's month-end close took ten working days, so management saw last month's numbers only in the third week of the next one; the goal was five working days within two quarters, without adding headcount.
**Action**: Mapped every close task and its owner; standardised the balance-sheet reconciliations in one template; moved accruals and intercompany matching earlier in the calendar; agreed cut-off dates with sales and operations; ran a weekly review of the slowest steps.
**Result**: Close down to five working days in the second quarter; audit adjustments fell from 14 to 3 a year; management meetings moved two weeks earlier.
**Learning**: The biggest gain came from agreeing cut-offs with the teams outside finance — the close was slow at its inputs, not in the finance team.

## Replaced the Annual Budget with a Rolling Forecast at Big Bank

**Context**: The retail division's annual budget was outdated by spring, and every quarter ended in a scramble of manual re-forecasts; the task was a rolling 18-month forecast that the division's managers would actually use.
**Action**: Designed the forecast around six drivers the managers already tracked; built the model and its Power BI report; ran workshops with each region to agree the drivers; kept the old budget in parallel for one year to compare.
**Result**: Forecast accuracy improved from ±12% to ±4% on quarterly costs; re-forecasting time dropped from three weeks to four days a quarter; the bank rolled the approach out to two more divisions.
**Learning**: Building the forecast on drivers the managers already owned made it their tool rather than finance's report — adoption followed ownership.

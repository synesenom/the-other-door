## Halved the close at Example Industries

**Context**: The close took ten days and management saw numbers late.
**Action**: Mapped every task, standardised reconciliations and agreed cut-offs.
**Result**: Close down to six days; audit adjustments fell from 14 to 3.
**Learning**: The gain came from the teams outside finance.

## Built a rolling forecast at Sample Bank

**Context**: The annual budget was outdated by spring.
**Action**: Designed a driver-based forecast and ran workshops in each region.
**Result**: Accuracy improved from 12 to 4 percent.
**Learning**: Ownership by the managers made it their tool.

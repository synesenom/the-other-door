Team Lead, Resourcing & Impact

ActionAid Denmark is looking for a strategic and collaborative Team Lead who can help more people find us, fund us and want to work with us.

ActionAid Denmark belongs to ActionAid International, a global federation that works with more than 25 million people in 45 countries for social justice, sustainable development and change driven by people themselves. Together with communities, partners and movements we take on poverty, inequality and injustice, and we now need a Team Lead who can tie our impact, our partnerships, our positioning and our income closer together than before.


About the role

The Team Lead, Resourcing and Impact, heads a team that covers resource mobilisation, strategic partnerships, monitoring, evaluation, accountability and learning, documentation and organisational learning. This is not a classic fundraising job. It is about making communication, engagement, impact documentation, foresight and fundraising pull in the same direction, so that ActionAid Denmark is better placed to raise money, build relationships and show the change we help bring about.

The role sits in AADK’s Programme Department and works with colleagues all over the organisation, the Engagement Department among them, to improve how we document and talk about impact, reach different audiences, sharpen our profile and spot the openings that appear around us. You will make sure that evidence, learning, relationships and stories come together in a way that keeps the organisation sustainable, accountable and influential.


Key responsibilities

  • Lead the development of strategic partnerships, the engagement of donors and our external positioning.
  • Drive the design and delivery of resource mobilisation strategies, including new funding sources and new markets.
  • Oversee the fundraising pipeline, the writing of proposals, donor reporting and documentation.
  • Strengthen the organisation’s MEAL systems, the way we generate evidence of impact and the way we learn from it.
  • Work with communication, engagement and programme colleagues to make AADK’s work easier to see, more convincing and more accessible to different audiences.
  • Set up and keep running good systems for sharing knowledge and a common place to learn.
  • Support joint learning with partners, grantees, donors and colleagues across the ActionAid federation.
  • Use strategic foresight to pick up trends, risks and opportunities that can shape fundraising, positioning and programme work.
  • Give strategic, operational and people leadership to the 5-person Resources & Impact team, covering planning, performance, professional development and wellbeing.
  • Make sure the team’s portfolio is run with sound financial management, risk management, compliance and quality assurance.
  • Take an active part in the leadership of the Programme Cluster, in organisational learning and in work across the organisation.


Key Deliverables

You will know you have succeeded when our funding comes from more sources, our donor relationships are strong, MEAL and reporting work well, and colleagues can easily find what we have learned and share solid evidence with the outside world.


What we are looking for

We want a leader who brings people, systems and ideas together. You know resource mobilisation from the inside, and you also see that fundraising rests on good evidence, clear stories, relationships built on trust, strategic communication and an organisation that is ready. You can lead when things are complex, get teams to agree and turn strategy into priorities, workplans and results that can be measured.

  • Solid, proven experience with resource mobilisation, strategic partnerships, donor engagement and writing proposals.
  • A strong grasp of MEAL, impact documentation, knowledge management and organisational learning.
  • Experience leading remote teams that span disciplines and cultures, and helping people grow through coaching, feedback and clear accountability.
  • A proven ability to work strategically with positioning, communication and audience engagement.
  • Strong stakeholder management and the ability to build good working relationships inside and outside the organisation.
  • Experience with donor compliance, reporting, financial oversight and risk management.
  • Excellent communication and facilitation skills in English (Danish a plus, but not required).
  • A collaborative and adaptable way of leading, and an appetite for learning.
  • Commitment to feminist, rights-based and locally led approaches to social change.


Location and reporting line

You report to the Director of Programmes, Rikke Holm, who is based in Germany, and you lead 5 colleagues working on strategic partnerships, MEAL and knowledge management from 4 different countries. The role can be based in Copenhagen, New York City or Washington D.C. We may also consider candidates elsewhere if they are close to major institutional donors and foundations; your application must explain why the location you propose makes strategic sense. You must already hold the residence and work permits needed in the country where you propose to be employed.


Why join us?

You get the chance to build a function that is central to ActionAid Denmark’s future, its relevance and its influence. You will work with dedicated colleagues in programmes, engagement, learning and partnerships to change how AADK and the wider ActionAid federation are seen and understood, and how we engage with the world around us. If social justice, strategic collaboration and the challenge of linking evidence, engagement and resources for more impact are what drive you, we would like to hear from you.

We offer a locally contracted leadership role with real influence on resource mobilisation, organisational learning, team development and AADK’s external profile. You will join a mission-driven team that works closely together and puts professional development and learning first. Salary, benefits and terms of employment follow the labour law and market practice where you are employed.

Indicative salary range:

New York: approx. USD 110,000–140,000 gross annual salary, plus benefits according to local terms

Copenhagen: DKK 54,000–66,000 gross monthly salary incl. Pension (set by the local AC collective agreement, based on experience and seniority).


How to Apply

Apply through our website with your CV and a short motivation letter that shows how your experience fits what we are looking for.

Application deadline: 13 October, 23:59 (CEST)

If you have questions about the position, please contact the Director of Programmes, Rikke Holm, at kontakt@example.com.

Commercial Category Manager – Commercial Contracts & Supplier Relations

Help Shape the Next Stage of Procurement at Contura

Contura International is expanding, and Procurement is moving from a support function to a strategic partner for the business, for our suppliers and for the plans we have for the years ahead.

There is real room to build a stronger supplier base, sharpen our commercial agreements, put clear governance in place and grow lasting partnerships with the suppliers that matter most to us.

To lead that work, we are looking for a Commercial Category Manager with broad experience in commercial contracts and negotiation, backed by a sound grasp of the legal side of commercial deals. You do well where the path is not mapped out in advance.

The Role

As Commercial Category Manager, you will lead the negotiation and day-to-day management of our commercial supplier agreements, and you will look after strategic supplier relationships and sourcing in a set of selected categories.

About 60% of your time goes to commercial contract management and supplier relationship management: negotiating contracts, running governance, developing suppliers and handling commercial risk. The other 40% is category management and strategic sourcing, making sure our supplier solutions are competitive, compliant and sustainable.

You will work side by side with the CFO, who carries the overall commercial responsibility, and the CLO, who handles Contura’s legal matters. You will also work with colleagues in Finance, QA, RA and R&D to make sure supplier performance, security of supply and our commercial agreements meet the needs of the business.

You will report directly to our Supply Chain Manager, Rikke Holm.

Key Responsibilities

Commercial Contract Management & Supplier Relationship Management

  • Lead commercial negotiations and agree our contractual positions with the CFO, the CLO and other stakeholders.
  • Write, review, negotiate and manage supplier contracts from the first draft to renewal or exit.
  • Use your working knowledge of commercial law and bring in the CLO for advice, assessment and sign-off when needed.
  • Build better contract templates, commercial governance and supplier management processes together with the CFO and CLO.
  • Develop lasting partnerships with strategic suppliers through clear communication, shared forecasts and long-term planning.
  • Run supplier reviews, development programmes and performance improvements with the people involved.
  • Work closely with Supplier Quality Management on supplier risk, quality requirements and continuity of supply.

Category Management & Strategic Sourcing

  • Run sourcing projects, from RFQs and RFPs to evaluation, supplier selection and a clear recommendation.
  • Give sourcing input to NPD, transfer projects, business changes and forced changes.
  • Take part in forecasting, capacity planning and long-term supplier planning, and bring Procurement into projects early.

Professional Qualifications

We imagine that you bring:

  • A relevant Master’s degree in Procurement, Commercial Law or a similar field.
  • 5+ years of relevant experience, including real responsibility for commercial contracts, supplier negotiations and the full contract lifecycle.
  • A proven record of leading complex commercial negotiations to fair agreements that create value for both sides.
  • A good working knowledge of the principles of commercial law
  • Experience with supplier relationships, supplier performance, sourcing processes and category strategies.
  • A background in manufacturing, MedTech, pharma, biotech or another regulated industry.
  • Fluent English communication skills; Danish is considered an advantage.

Personal Profile

You like to bring order to things, and you get them done.

You are at ease in a growing company where many structures are still being built.

You pair sound commercial judgement and strong negotiation skills with a practical feel for the legal side of a deal. You know when to call in legal expertise, and you can weigh value, risk and the needs of the business against each other.

You take the initiative, think analytically and look for solutions, and you take ownership of your results. You speak with confidence to senior stakeholders, and you are a team player who earns trust and builds good relationships inside and outside the company.

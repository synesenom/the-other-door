Vos missions en quelques mots

The expert will support a bilateral research centre in New Delhi and help strengthen the way its committees are run.

Main tasks and expected outcomes:

· Take part in the meetings of the scientific and governing committees.

· Help prepare the annual work plan and budget.

· Advise the staff on reporting to both funding governments.

Profil recherché

You have at least eight years of experience in research programme management, with a good knowledge of the Indian science system.

· Excellent spoken and written English.

· Willingness to live in New Delhi for the length of the assignment.

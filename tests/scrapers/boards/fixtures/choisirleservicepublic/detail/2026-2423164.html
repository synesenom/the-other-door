Vos missions en quelques mots

The Grant Desk Coordinator will follow the European funding landscape and flag calls that fit the university's research strategy.

Main duties:

· Prepare and review funding proposals together with the research teams.

· Keep a calendar of deadlines and coordinate internal approvals.

· Report each term to the research office on the success rate of submissions.

Profil recherché

You hold a master's degree and have at least two years of experience supporting grant applications, ideally for Horizon Europe.

Fluent English is required; French is an advantage.

· Strong writing and project management skills.

· Comfortable working with researchers from many disciplines.

IDT Change Manager

The closing date is 17 November 2026

Job summary

The IDT department is looking for a change manager to join its change team on a fixed term contract of twelve months.

You will help staff across the Trust adopt new electronic record tools and the pathways built on them, and keep disruption to a minimum.

Main duties of the job

You will study current processes and requirements and help design the documentation that the change needs. In your work-streams you will:

  • Agree the scope and deliverables of each change request.
  • Compare existing processes with the Trust requirements.
  • Plan communications with clinical, managerial and administrative staff.
  • Work with the change agents and senior change analysts of the team.

About us

The hospitals group serves a population of four million people in south west London and north east Surrey and is a major teaching hospital.

Job description

Job responsibilities

  • Work with staff in each area to find out which changes are needed.
  • Run a gap analysis between today's processes and those of the new system, and write the downtime documents.
  • Line manage the change agents and oversee their workload.

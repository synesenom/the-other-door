Postdoctoral researcher in Vaccine Development

Orebro

Reference number ORU 2.1.1-06109/2026

The School of Medical Sciences is seeking a postdoctoral researcher in Biomedicine with a focus on molecular virology and vaccine development.

Subject area

The subject area for this position is Biomedicine.

Background

The position is part of a research project on new mRNA-based vaccines, funded by a foundation. The research builds on long experience of virus biology and of how viruses interact with their hosts.

Duties and responsibilities

The successful candidate will carry out experimental research on the molecular biology of tick-borne viruses and take part in designing and evaluating vaccine candidates with cell-based assays and animal models.

Qualifications

To be eligible, applicants must hold a doctoral degree, or an equivalent foreign degree, in a relevant subject. Priority is given to applicants who obtained their degree no more than three years before the application deadline.

Information

The position is a full-time fixed-term appointment for two years, preferably starting in January. For further information, please contact Professor Rikke Holm, e-mail: kontakt@example.com

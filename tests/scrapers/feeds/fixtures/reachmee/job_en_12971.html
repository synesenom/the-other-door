We are looking for a research assistant in biomedicine for a temporary position in a research environment studying cardiovascular disease at the Department of Medical Sciences.

Background

The research environment carries out translational research in the cardiovascular field and brings together groups that study molecular mechanisms in blood vessels. The work is part of a four-year project on new drug candidates.

Duties and responsibilities

The duties include cell and molecular biology studies of how inflammation and the immune response are regulated in vascular cells. Some teaching and supervision may be included.

Qualifications

You must hold a Bachelor's degree in biomedicine or a related field and have documented practical experience of cell culture, isolation of nucleic acids and real-time PCR.

Roofer Apprentice

The Vacancy

To train as a roofer, installing and maintaining roofs and carrying out the other tasks of your trade on time and to a high standard, while giving excellent service to residents and clients.

As an apprentice you will learn how to work safely at height, read drawings and use the tools and materials of the trade, supported by an experienced mentor and by college study one day a week.

Work for Wates

Wates is a family-owned development, building and property maintenance company with a long history in the built environment.

We are guided by our purpose, and we care about our people, our customers and the places where we work. Contact recruitment@example.com with any question.

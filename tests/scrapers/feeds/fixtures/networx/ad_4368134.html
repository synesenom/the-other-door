Bank Lecturer (Adult Learning)

The Vacancy

Contract Type: Bank / Hourly Paid

About the Role

We are recruiting Bank Lecturers to teach and assess on our adult learning courses. You will encourage and support adult students as they build their skills and move towards their next step in study or work.

You will plan lessons that suit a wide range of learners, mark work, give clear feedback and keep accurate records of progress and attendance.

About You

You hold, or are working towards, a teaching qualification, and have recent experience of teaching adults in further education or training.
